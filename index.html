About this page

This is a student-run resource for the Department of Zoology, Banaras Hindu University. It isn't an official university platform — it exists because sharing notes and specimen references helps everyone in the batch. Treat it the way you'd treat notes passed around in class: with courtesy, and without lifting copyrighted material into it.
