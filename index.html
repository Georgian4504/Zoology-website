🔒

Files you share here are added for classmates and the department — please don't upload material you don't have the right to share, and keep specimen photos to your own lab work.

Before you upload

  • 🧾 Name your files clearly — e.g. "Cell Biology Unit 3 – Notes.pdf" helps others find it fast.
  • 🔬 Specimen photos should be your own dissection or lab work, in focus, with the paper/practical noted in the filename.
  • 🚫 No copyrighted textbooks or scans of paid material — this shelf is for original student notes and department resources only.
  • 🤝 One folder, everyone's notes — check the drive first so we don't end up with duplicates.
