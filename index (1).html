A shared shelf for Zoology, built by the department

Notes, papers, specimen references and thesis material — organised in one place so every student in the department can find what they need, and add what they have.

Shared for study, not for sale

Everything on this shelf is for the academic use of Zoology students at BHU. Please don't sell, rebrand, or redistribute this material outside the department, and don't upload anything you don't have the right to share. Respecting that keeps the shelf open for everyone.

About the shelf

Built by the department, for the department

Who it's for

Zoology students at Banaras Hindu University looking for a single, organised place to find and share notes, papers, and specimen material across the subject.

How the shelf grows

Anyone in the department can contribute. Files added through the drive folder become part of the shared collection for the whole batch to use.

Fair use

Material here is for study within the department only. Please keep it that way — no reselling, no external redistribution, no removing credit from original authors.
